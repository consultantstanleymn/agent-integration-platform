# OutdoorCo Returns Policy

## Return window

OutdoorCo accepts returns within 30 days of the delivery date for most products. A customer must start a return before the 30 day window closes. Items marked final sale cannot be returned. Items purchased on the OutdoorCo website and in OutdoorCo retail stores follow the same window.

## Condition and proof of purchase

Returned items must be unused and in original packaging. Tents, backpacks, stoves, filters, and lanterns must include all original parts. Hiking boots and rain jackets must not show mud, wear, or damage.
Customers must provide a receipt, order number, or packing slip. A customer without proof of purchase may still receive store credit based on the lowest selling price in the past 90 days.

## Return shipping and RMA

A customer must request an RMA before sending any return. The RMA number must be written on the outside of the package. Packages without an RMA may be refused at the warehouse.
OutdoorCo pays return shipping for items that arrived defective or were sent by OutdoorCo in error. For all other returns, the customer pays return shipping. Return labels are provided in the RMA email when OutdoorCo is responsible.

## Restocking fee rules

A restocking fee of 15 percent applies to opened STV-77 camp stoves, FLT-3 water filters, and LNT-66 lanterns. A restocking fee of 10 percent applies to opened TNT-4421 and TNT-5530 tents.
The restocking fee is deducted from the refund. The fee is waived when the item is defective or OutdoorCo shipped the wrong SKU.

## Refund timing

OutdoorCo issues a refund after the warehouse receives and inspects the item. Inspection usually takes 2 business days. The refund is submitted within 5 business days after inspection.
Credit card refunds may take 5 to 10 business days to appear on a statement. Store credit refunds are available immediately after processing.

## Exchanges and replacements

Customers can request an exchange instead of a refund for the same item in a different size or color. Exchanges must be started within 30 days of delivery. Once the returned item passes inspection, OutdoorCo ships the exchange without an extra shipping fee.
If the desired item is out of stock, the customer chooses between a refund and store credit.
