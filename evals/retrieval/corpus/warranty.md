# OutdoorCo Warranty Policy

## Warranty coverage

OutdoorCo warrants products against defects in materials and workmanship under normal outdoor use. The warranty applies to the original purchaser only. This warranty does not extend to normal wear, accident damage, or improper care. Proof of purchase may be required for any warranty claim.

## Covered models

The following product codes have these warranty lengths from the date of purchase:
- TNT-4421, 4 person tent, 2 years.
- TNT-5530, 2 person tent, 2 years.
- PCK-310, 50 litre pack, 5 years.
- STV-77, camp stove, 3 years.
- BTS-208, hiking boots, 1 year.
- JKT-905, rain jacket, 2 years.
- SLP-12, sleeping bag, 5 years.
- FLT-3, water filter, 1 year.
- LNT-66, lantern, 2 years.
- POL-9, trekking poles, 3 years.

## Warranty period and proof

The warranty period begins on the purchase date. Customers must provide an order number, receipt, or the SKU label from the product. If no proof is available, OutdoorCo may use the manufacturing date to estimate coverage.
Warranty periods are not restarted when a replacement item is issued. A replacement inherits the remaining period of the original purchase.

## Defect claims

A customer reports a defect by contacting OutdoorCo support and describing the problem with photos. For stoves and water filters, support may ask for a short video of the issue before approval.
Approved warranty claims receive a replacement, repair, or comparable model at OutdoorCo's choice. OutdoorCo does not refund cash for a warranty defect.

## Warranty exclusions

The warranty excludes damage from falls, exposure to corrosive liquids, misuse, and unauthorized repair. It also excludes normal cosmetic wear on boots, packs, and jackets. Tents damaged by extreme wind above the product rating are excluded.
Consumable parts such as filter cartridges and lantern mantles have no separate warranty beyond the original product period.

## Repair and replacement options

OutdoorCo pays standard ground shipping for approved replacement items sent within the first year of a warranty claim. After the first year, the customer pays shipping to the warranty centre and OutdoorCo pays return ground shipping.
If repair is needed, OutdoorCo aims to complete it within 15 business days of receiving the item. When parts are not available, OutdoorCo may replace with an equivalent current model.
