# Price adjustment policy

## Price adjustment request window

A customer may request a price adjustment within 14 days of the shipment date.

The new lower price must be the current price on the OutdoorCo website. The customer receives the difference once per item.

The shipment date is the date the carrier first scans the package.

## Qualifying price adjustment items

The item must be the same SKU, color, size, and condition as the original purchase.

The item must be in stock when the request is reviewed. For example, PCK-310 purchased at full price qualifies if the same 50 litre pack SKU drops in price.

A price adjustment is not a return and does not create a new order.

## Price match exclusions

OutdoorCo does not price match auction sites, membership clubs, marketplace sellers, or third party coupon codes.

The policy excludes bundle promotions, rental fees, repair services, gift cards, and items bought during a flash sale or doorbuster. A price match request does not stack with a price adjustment.

Price match requests must be for an identical item that is available for immediate purchase.

## Price adjustment refund method

An approved adjustment returns to the original payment method within 5 business days.

If the original payment was store credit or a gift card, the adjustment returns as store credit. If a credit card was used, the refund goes back to that card.

Agents must not issue a price adjustment as cash.

## Price adjustment and loyalty points

When a price adjustment lowers an item price, the loyalty points earned on that item are recalculated.

If the member already redeemed those points, the point portion of the adjustment is returned as store credit. The member account is updated within 24 hours.

Points are never refunded as an external payment method.

## Price adjustment processing timeline

Agents may process a price adjustment of less than 100 dollars after verifying the order and current price.

Price adjustments of 100 dollars or more require team lead review. Most price adjustment requests are completed within 2 business days.

The customer receives a confirmation email when the adjustment is approved.
