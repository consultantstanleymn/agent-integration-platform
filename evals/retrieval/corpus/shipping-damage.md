# OutdoorCo Shipping Damage Policy

## Damage reporting window

Customers must report visible shipping damage within 5 calendar days of delivery. Reports made after 5 days may still be reviewed, but OutdoorCo may not be able to file a carrier claim. Damage reports go to OutdoorCo support with the order number.

## Photo evidence requirements

The customer must provide clear photos of the damaged product, the inside packing material, and all sides of the outer box. At least one photo must show the product code label. Missing photos may delay the review by 2 business days.
If the item is unsafe to handle, the customer should not move it and should describe the damage in writing.

## Waybill inspection

The customer should write "damaged on arrival" on the waybill when a box shows visible damage on delivery. If the carrier refuses to accept the note, the customer should still take a photo of the waybill before the driver leaves. OutdoorCo uses the waybill photo as supporting proof for the claim.

## Replacement shipment

Once the damage review is approved, OutdoorCo ships a replacement within 2 business days if stock is available. If stock is not available, the customer chooses store credit or a refund. OutdoorCo sends a prepaid return label if the damaged item must be returned.
For destroyed tents or packs, OutdoorCo may instruct the customer to dispose of the item after the review.

## Missing items and carrier notes

Customers must report missing items within 7 calendar days of delivery. OutdoorCo checks the package weight and carrier scan before issuing a refund or replacement.
If a package arrives open or resealed, the customer should note that on the waybill and take a photo before opening the box.

## Customer responsibility after delivery

Customers must keep the original packaging and product until the claim is closed. OutdoorCo may ask for the packaging up to 10 business days after the report. If the customer discards the box or waybill, OutdoorCo may deny the claim.
For porch theft after delivery, OutdoorCo directs the customer to file a police report and then reviews replacement on a case basis.
