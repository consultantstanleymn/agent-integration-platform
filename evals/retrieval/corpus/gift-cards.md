# OutdoorCo Gift Card Policy

## Gift card purchase options

OutdoorCo sells digital and physical gift cards in amounts between 25 dollars and 500 dollars. Digital gift cards are delivered by email within 2 hours of purchase. Physical gift cards ship within 2 business days.
Gift cards can be purchased online, in OutdoorCo retail stores, or by phone through support.

## Gift card balance and redemption

Gift cards can be used for products, standard shipping, and taxes on the OutdoorCo website and in stores. The balance is reduced after each transaction. A customer can check a gift card balance online or by calling support.
Gift cards do not expire and carry no monthly fees.

## Gift card refunds and chargebacks

Gift cards are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged, except when required by law. If a customer files a chargeback for a gift card purchase, OutdoorCo freezes the linked gift card balance until the dispute is resolved.
Successful chargebacks may result in the gift card balance being voided. Customers should contact support before starting a chargeback for a missing digital card.

## Lost or stolen gift cards

A lost physical gift card can be replaced only when the customer provides the original purchase receipt and the card number. Replacement value equals the remaining balance at the time of the report. Lost digital gift cards can be resent to the original email after identity verification.
OutdoorCo is not responsible for gift card balances spent after loss unless the card was frozen before use.

## Promotional gift cards

Promotional gift cards from offers may have a shorter expiry period. The offer will show the expiry, usually 90 days from issue. Promotional gift cards cannot be extended or converted to cash.
Promotional gift cards apply to merchandise only and cannot be combined with other promotional codes.

## Gift card exclusions

Gift cards cannot be used to purchase other gift cards, rental gear reservations, or price matching deposits. They also cannot be used for warranty repair shipping costs.
OutdoorCo reserves the right to limit gift card use when fraud is suspected.
