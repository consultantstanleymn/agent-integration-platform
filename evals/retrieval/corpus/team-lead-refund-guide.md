# Team Lead Refund Guide

## Refund authority limits

A team lead may approve a refund of up to 300 dollars per order without escalation. A senior team lead may approve up to 1000 dollars. Refunds above 1000 dollars require a manager review.
The limit includes the product total and any shipping refund. It does not include duty or customs amounts, which OutdoorCo does not refund.

## Override approval steps

A team lead override follows these steps: verify the customer identity, check the order history, review the item condition, and document the business reason. After that, the team lead applies the override in the order system.
If the refund is above the team lead limit, the team lead submits an escalation with the same evidence and waits for written approval.

## High-risk refund review

Team leads must review high-risk refunds for indicators from the fraud signals reference. A high-risk refund includes a new account, a large order over 600 dollars, or a shipping address changed after purchase.
If the order is high risk, the team lead places the refund on hold and escalates to fraud operations before release.

## Refund exception documentation

Document every refund exception with the order number, product code, reason, and approval level. A missing document may result in the refund being reversed in the internal audit.
Use the free text field for the customer conversation summary. Keep the summary factual and under 300 words.

## Team lead hold and release

A team lead may hold a refund for up to 3 business days for review. Holds above 3 business days require a manager approval. The customer must be told about the hold timeline in the first reply.
Once a hold is resolved, the refund must be released the same business day.

## Coaching notes for refund decisions

Support agents should not promise an override before a team lead reviews it. A team lead should coach agents to explain the return and refund policy without creating an exception expectation.
Use past decisions only as examples when they match the current order facts.

## Emergency refund procedure

If a customer received a defective item and needs immediate gear for a trip, a team lead may approve a replacement without waiting for the return. The replacement must be limited to one item per order and the original item must still be returned or documented.
For safety-related defects, use the emergency refund procedure and notify the product safety team within one business day.
