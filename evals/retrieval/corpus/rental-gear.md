# Rental gear policy

## Rental eligibility and age requirement

Rental customers must be at least 18 years old and show a valid government photo ID.

A credit card must be on file for the rental period. The rental period begins on the start date selected at checkout and ends at closing time on the return date.

The customer must sign a rental agreement before equipment leaves the store.

## Rental deposit authorization holds

OutdoorCo places an authorization hold on the rental credit card, not a final charge.

The hold is 200 dollars for TNT-4421, 200 dollars for TNT-5530, 100 dollars for STV-77, 75 dollars for SLP-12, and 50 dollars for BTS-208. The hold is released within 24 hours after the returned item passes inspection.

If a hold is converted to a charge, the customer receives a receipt and a written reason.

## Damage waiver coverage

The damage waiver costs 8 dollars per rental day and covers accidental damage to the rented item.

The waiver does not cover loss, theft, or intentional misuse. Without the waiver, the customer pays the repair cost up to the full replacement value.

A damage waiver must be selected at checkout and cannot be added after the rental period begins.

## Late return rental fees

A late return fee is 15 dollars per day for each day past the return date.

If the rental is not returned by day 14, the item is treated as lost and the full replacement cost is charged. Late fees are charged in addition to the daily rental fee.

Agents should call the customer on the first late day before charging the late fee.

## Cleaning and missing parts charges

A cleaning fee of 20 dollars applies if rented gear is returned wet, muddy, or with food residue.

Missing parts such as tent stakes, stove grates, or lantern mantles are charged at their current listed price. The customer can avoid a cleaning fee by wiping down equipment before return.

A customer who disagrees with a cleaning charge may ask for a photo review by a team lead.

## Rental gear exclusions and maximum period

The maximum rental period is 14 consecutive days. Extensions require a new reservation and a returned item check.

The FLT-3 water filter and POL-9 trekking poles are not available for rental due to hygiene and wear concerns. The LNT-66 lantern is available for rental with a 30 dollar hold and a 5 dollar daily fee.
