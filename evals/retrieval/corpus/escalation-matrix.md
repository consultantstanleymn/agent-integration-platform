# Escalation matrix

## Escalation matrix scope

This policy applies to refunds, replacements, store credit, and chargeback responses. Agents must apply the limit that matches their role.

Every escalation must include the order ID, customer email, product code, requested amount, and a short reason.

Agents who are unsure about a limit must contact the shift team lead before taking action.

## Tier one refund and replacement limit

An agent may approve a refund up to 100 dollars or a replacement up to 150 dollars for one order without team lead review.

The approval must be logged in the order record. Agents must not split one order into smaller approval requests to stay under the limit.

This limit also applies to return label fees and shipping cost refunds for verified order errors.

## Tier two team lead review limit

A team lead may approve a refund up to 300 dollars or a replacement up to 400 dollars for one order.

The team lead must check order history, return history, and item condition before approval. For returns older than 30 days, the team lead may require photos.

Team lead approval must be recorded in the order history with the same order ID.

## Tier three manager review limit

A manager may approve a refund up to 1000 dollars or store credit up to 1200 dollars for one order.

Requests above those amounts move to the finance team for review. A manager may also approve an exception when a customer has a verified product defect.

Manager approval is required when refunds and previous concessions for the same customer total more than 1500 dollars over 12 months.

## Chargeback escalation path

Agents must forward any chargeback notice or bank inquiry to the finance team within 1 business day.

Do not issue a new refund for an order that already has an open chargeback unless finance approves it in writing. Finance aims to respond to card network inquiries within 5 business days.

Finance may request the original packing slip and proof of delivery before responding.

## Emergency field safety path

A report of fire, burn, structural failure, water contamination, or serious injury must be escalated to a manager and the safety inbox within 4 hours.

The agent stops normal troubleshooting and collects the lot code, product code, and clear photos if available. The manager contacts the customer and follows the safety review process.

Do not request a standard return for a safety report until the manager completes the safety review.
