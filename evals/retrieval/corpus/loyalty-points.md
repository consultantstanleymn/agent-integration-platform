# Loyalty points policy

## Earning loyalty points on qualifying purchases

Members earn 1 point for each full 1 dollar spent on qualifying merchandise.

Qualifying merchandise excludes tax, shipping, gift cards, rental fees, repair charges, and store credit used at checkout. Points post to the member account 14 days after the order ships.

Points do not post for canceled orders or for items returned before the posting date.

## Loyalty point value and redemption minimum

One loyalty point has a value of 1 cent toward future purchases.

The minimum redemption is 100 points at checkout. Points cannot be used to buy gift cards or to pay tax or shipping.

Redeemed points reduce the subtotal before tax and shipping are calculated.

## Loyalty points on returns and refunds

When an item purchased with points is returned, the cash portion refunds to the original payment method and the redeemed point value returns as store credit.

If a returned item earned points, those points are deducted from the member balance. The deduction may make the balance negative for future earning.

A negative point balance recovers through points earned on later qualifying purchases.

## Loyalty point expiration rules

Points expire after 24 consecutive months with no earning or redemption activity.

Any single earning or redemption activity resets the 24 month expiration period. Points are removed immediately when a member closes the account.

Expired points cannot be restored, except when an account had a documented billing error.

## Loyalty point account adjustments

A member may request a missing point review within 60 days of the shipment date.

Agents may add up to 500 points if the member provides a receipt and a valid SKU. Adjustments above 1000 points require team lead review.

A point adjustment must include the order ID and the exact number of points posted.

## Loyalty point transfer restrictions

Loyalty points cannot be transferred, sold, combined, inherited, or moved between accounts.

Points have no cash value except where a legal requirement applies. OutdoorCo may cancel points from accounts that use false purchase records.

Joint accounts are not offered for loyalty points.
