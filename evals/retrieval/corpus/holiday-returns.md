# Holiday return policy

## Holiday return window date range

Items shipped between November 1 and December 24 may be returned through January 31.

The item must be unused and in its original packaging when possible. The standard return window resumes on February 1 for orders shipped after December 24.

Orders shipped before November 1 do not receive the extended holiday window.

## Gift receipt holiday return handling

A gift return made between December 26 and January 31 with a gift receipt receives store credit equal to the price listed on the gift receipt.

If there is no gift receipt, the refund is store credit at the lowest sale price from the previous 60 days. Store credit from a holiday gift return is loaded onto a store credit code after the return scan.

Agents must not reveal the original purchase price when a customer has a gift receipt.

## Holiday return condition checks

Holiday return items must have tags attached and no dirt, odor, or visible wear.

Footwear must show no outdoor wear. Tents, sleeping bags, and water filters must be clean and dry before the return is accepted.

Agents may refuse a holiday return if the item shows signs of use beyond indoor trial.

## Holiday restocking fee waiver

No restocking fee applies during the extended holiday return window.

After January 31, a restocking fee of 5 percent may apply to used tents, sleeping bags, stoves, and footwear under the standard policy.

The restocking fee is based on the item price before tax and shipping.

## Holiday return shipping labels

For a wrong item or shipping damage, OutdoorCo provides a prepaid waybill and return label.

For a change-of-mind holiday return, the customer pays return shipping unless the item shipped after December 10 and arrived damaged.

A waybill from the original carrier may be reused only when OutdoorCo created the label.

## Holiday return processing time

A warehouse return scan occurs within 5 business days of delivery back to the returns center.

Store credit is issued within 1 business day after the scan. Refunds to a credit card take 3 to 5 business days after the scan.

Peak volume in early January may add 2 business days to the warehouse scan time.
