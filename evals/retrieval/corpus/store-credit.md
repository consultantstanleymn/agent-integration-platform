# OutdoorCo Store Credit Policy

## Store credit issuance

OutdoorCo issues store credit when a customer returns an item after the 30 day return window but within 60 days of delivery. Store credit is also issued for returns without proof of purchase if the item passes inspection.
Store credit is loaded to the customer's OutdoorCo account. It can also be sent as a code by email.

## Gift receipt returns

A return with a gift receipt always receives store credit for the original purchase price. The store credit is issued to the person returning the item. The gift receiver does not need the order number of the original buyer.
Gift receipt returns must still meet the condition rules in the returns policy. Final sale items remain ineligible.

## Store credit balance and use

Store credit can be used on the OutdoorCo website and at OutdoorCo retail stores. It can cover product costs and standard shipping. It cannot cover gift card purchases or sales tax in some regions.
Store credit balances are displayed in the customer account. A customer can also check a balance by entering the code in the payment step.

## Store credit expiry and fees

Store credit expires 24 months after the issue date. OutdoorCo does not charge maintenance fees or dormancy fees. A refund returned to store credit does not extend the original expiry date.
If a customer has multiple store credit codes, the oldest code is used first unless the customer selects another code.

## Store credit lookup

Support agents can look up store credit using the customer email, order number, or the store credit code. If the customer cannot find the code, support verifies identity with the billing address and order history.
A lost store credit code can be reissued once when the original email is confirmed. Reissued codes retain the original expiry date.

## Combining store credit with other payments

Store credit can be combined with a credit card, debit card, or one OutdoorCo gift card on a single order. Two store credit codes can be combined when they are both active.
Store credit cannot be applied to an order that is already paid or to a purchase of another store credit code.
