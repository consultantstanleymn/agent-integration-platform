# Repair service policy

## Repair service intake steps

The customer submits an order ID or proof of purchase, the product code, a short issue description, and photos.

For BTS-208 hiking boots, photos of the outsole, midsole, and upper are required. For TNT-4421 and TNT-5530 tents, photos of the poles, zippers, and fabric are required.

The repair team sends an initial assessment within 2 business days.

## Diagnostic fee and approval rules

The diagnostic fee is 25 dollars per item and is paid before repair work begins.

The fee is waived when the approved repair labor and parts total 50 dollars or more. If the item cannot be repaired, the diagnostic fee is returned as store credit.

The customer must approve the repair estimate before parts are ordered.

## Repair warranty period

Approved repairs are covered for 90 days from the date the repaired item ships back to the customer.

If the same defect repeats within 90 days, OutdoorCo repairs it again free, including parts and labor. The customer pays shipping for repeat repairs after 90 days.

The warranty covers only the repaired defect, not new damage.

## Repair lead time and parts

The standard repair turnaround is 10 business days after the customer approves the repair estimate.

If a required part is not in stock, the repair may take 15 additional business days and the customer is notified. Rush repair service is not offered.

Customers may check repair status through the order status page.

## Customer shipping responsibilities

The customer pays shipping to the repair center. OutdoorCo pays return ground shipping for completed repairs.

After the diagnostic fee is paid, the repair team sends an RMA number and return waybill instructions. The customer must write the RMA number on the outside of the box.

A repair box without an RMA number may be returned unopened.

## Items not accepted for repair

OutdoorCo does not repair items with biohazard contamination, severe mold, or live insects.

The FLT-3 water filter is not repaired because it is a sealed hygiene item. A cracked fuel line on the STV-77 camp stove is not repaired; the customer receives a replacement offer if the stove is within warranty.
